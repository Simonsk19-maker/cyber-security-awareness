Staff Cyber Security Checklist

Simple habits for everyone, plus a list for new starters.

Every day

  • Lock your screen when you step away (Windows key + L).
  • Pause on any email that rushes you, asks you to log in, or has an unexpected attachment.
  • Check the real sender before clicking; hover over links to see where they go.
  • Never share your password, and never type it into a page you reached from an email link.
  • Report anything odd to __________________ (your IT/manager contact) — it's never a bother.

Keep good habits

  • Use 2-factor login on your work accounts.
  • Use a password manager; don't reuse passwords.
  • Install updates when prompted (don't keep clicking “later”).
  • Only install apps from official stores.
  • On public Wi-Fi, avoid banking/admin — use mobile data for sensitive work.

Handling money & data

  • Verify any “change of bank details” by phone, on a number you already have.
  • Be wary of urgent, secret payment requests — check in person or by phone.
  • Only access the customer data you need; keep it on work systems.

New starter — first day

  • Set a strong, unique password and turn on 2-factor login.
  • Set up the password manager.
  • Know who to report security worries to.
  • Read the short guides at this site's home page.
  • Understand what you may and may not install.
If something goes wrong: disconnect from Wi-Fi, tell your IT/manager contact straight away, and change your password from a clean device. Fast reporting limits the damage.
