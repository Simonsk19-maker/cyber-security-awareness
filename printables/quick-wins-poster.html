Top 10 Cyber Security Quick Wins

For small businesses. Pin it up or add it to your staff handbook.

  1. Turn on 2-factor login for email, Microsoft 365 / Google and banking. Stops most break-ins even if a password leaks.
  2. Use a password manager and three-random-word passwords. Never reuse your email password anywhere.
  3. Back up 3-2-1: 3 copies, 2 types, 1 off-site and disconnected. Test a restore now and then.
  4. Keep everything updated — turn on automatic updates for Windows, phones, browsers and Office.
  5. Think before you click. Urgency, a login request or an odd link = stop and check.
  6. Verify money changes by phone on a known number — especially “new bank details” or an urgent payment from “the boss”.
  7. Leave antivirus and the firewall on (Windows Defender is fine for many small firms).
  8. Encrypt and lock devices — BitLocker / FileVault, plus a PIN or password on every laptop and phone.
  9. Give least access. Not everyone needs to be an administrator; remove leavers' accounts promptly.
  10. Know how to report it. Scam emails → report@phishing.gov.uk · texts → 7726 · fraud → Action Fraud 0300 123 2040 (Scotland: 101).
