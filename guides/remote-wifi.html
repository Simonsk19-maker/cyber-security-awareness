Remote working & public Wi-Fi

Working safely from home, cafés and on the move.

Working from home, cafés or on the move is normal now — and fine, with a few sensible habits.

Public Wi-Fi

  • Assume open Wi-Fi (cafés, hotels, airports) could be watched. Avoid logging into banking or admin accounts on it.
  • Prefer your phone's mobile data / personal hotspot for anything sensitive.
  • Make sure websites show https and a padlock.
  • A VPN adds protection on untrusted networks — useful if staff work on the move a lot.

Working from home

  • Change the default admin password on the home router, and keep its firmware updated.
  • Keep work on work accounts and devices where you can; don't mix with the family PC.
  • Lock your screen when you step away (Windows key + L).
  • Turn on 2-factor login for anything you reach from home.

Lost or stolen device

  • Encryption + a screen lock means the finder can't read your data.
  • Use Find My / Find My Device to lock or wipe it remotely.
  • Tell whoever manages your IT so accounts can be secured.
